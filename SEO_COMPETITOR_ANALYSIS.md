## 2. Technical SEO & On-Page Comparison

### Competitor Strategy Breakdown
1. **Title Tag Formats:**
   - *LeewayHertz:* "AI Agent Development Company | Custom AI Agents - LeewayHertz" (Strict keyword-first title, high CTR).
   - *LuMay:* "Top AI Automation Agency | Custom Agentic Workflows"
   - *Artkai:* "AI Automation Services & Business Process Re-engineering | Artkai"
2. **Content Architecture:**
   - Top pages feature a clear 5-stage layout:
     - 1. Above-the-fold Hero with explicit commercial positioning + direct audit CTA.
     - 2. Concrete Architectural System Diagram (illustrating inputs, reasoning layer, API action executions).
     - 3. Tabular breakdown of Capabilities & Tech Stack (LangGraph, Python, Vector DBs, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, CRM webhooks).
     - 4. Real-world verticalized use cases (Real Estate, Healthcare, B2B Ops).
     - 5. Structured FAQs marked up with `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema for Google rich snippet carousels.
3. **Structured Data Implementation:**
   - Competitors heavily leverage `@type: Service`, `@type: Organization`, and `@type: FAQPage`.
   - Weakness: Most competitors do not implement connected `@graph` knowledge graphs or `SoftwareApplication` cross-links for their internal tools.
