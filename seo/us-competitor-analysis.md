## Observed winning patterns

1. **Country-fit pages:** US-oriented results explicitly explain location, delivery, and customer fit.
2. **Proof density:** stronger pages include named work, screenshots, case studies, certifications, or specific artifacts.
3. **Clear entry offers:** audits, roadmaps, proofs of concept, or fixed-scope starting points reduce ambiguity.
4. **Workflow specificity:** CRM updates, document intake, customer triage, and human approvals outperform generic transformation language.
5. **Evaluation content:** listicles and procurement guides occupy commercial SERPs alongside agency pages.

## Positioning recommendation

Arenova should not imitate a US-headquartered agency. Its defensible position is a founder-led remote engineering partner for businesses whose workflows have outgrown no-code chains: direct access to the builder, explicit permissions, validation, exception handling, staged release, and documented handover.
